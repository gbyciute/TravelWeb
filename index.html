Travel Journal

Discovering Madeira

Beautiful nature and mountains

Madeira is a beautiful island with amazing mountains, green forests and beautiful ocean views. It is a great destination for people who enjoy nature and adventure.

One of the best things to do in Madeira is walking through the famous hiking trails. You can also visit small villages and enjoy the local food.

Read more about Madeira

A Weekend in Lisbon

City, food and culture

Lisbon is a colourful city with beautiful streets, old buildings and famous yellow trams. It is a perfect place for a short city break.

During your trip you can visit historical places, try traditional Portuguese food and walk around the narrow streets of the city.

Discover Lisbon

Exploring Porto

A city by the river

Porto is a beautiful city located near the Douro River. The city is famous for its colourful buildings, bridges and traditional atmosphere.

Visitors can walk along the river, explore the old town and taste traditional Portuguese dishes. Porto is also a great place for people who love photography.

Visit Porto website

Adventure in Algarve

Beaches and mountains

Algarve is a great destination for adventurous travellers. The country has beautiful beaches, high mountains and a long coastline.

You can explore beautiful beaches, visit traditional villages and discover the beautiful landscapes. Algarve is also known for its friendly and welcoming people.
