Recommendations for travelers

Panama's out-of-the-way archipelago has stayed slow-paced even as it's grown into a hangout for backpackers making their way around Central America. It is a prime location to de-stress and work on your tan, but there's plenty to discover away from the beaches and hammocks. Expect water sports, wildlife and an inspired culinary scene.

With spectacular desert landscapes, soaring mountains and a long coastline, Oman is a joy for adventurous travelers. But the most memorable part of a trip here is meeting the hospitable Omani people.

Auto Rentals

We recommend compact or mid-sized cars because they are much easier to maneuver through narrow country lanes and tight parking spaces in historic towns. If you plan on visiting the Scottish Highlands, a slightly larger vehicle with more power is helpful for navigating steeper hills and winding roads.
